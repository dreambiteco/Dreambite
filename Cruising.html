CRUISING

by Katrina Bennett

A sizzling, subversive romcom about polyamory, protest, and passion in a warming world.

London Run (Coming Soon)

When a dancer and a climate activist meet over margaritas on a Mediterranean cruise, their sizzling attraction seems like the perfect holiday fling. Until the pair realise they share more than just chemistry: both are in a relationship with the same person.

As potential romance explodes into fierce rivalry, the duo launch a competitive campaign of eco-sabotage against the mega-polluting ship. With the heat rising, can their connection and desire for a better world survive these dangerous waters?

During a year where protest has exploded globally, Cruising is a necessary and subversive exploration of both passion and protest. Beneath its warm romcom narrative, this play has a fierce underbelly. It urgently spotlights the collision of authoritarian anti-protest laws with impending climate collapse. More than that, in its connection between polyamory and the climate crisis, Cruising asks pertinent questions and provokes conversations about how our current systems have failed, challenging audiences to rebel in favour of community and human connection.

Reviews

What is impressive is that the play never treats politics and intimacy as two separate subjects. It tells us that our political beliefs often enter our private lives: into the people we love, the compromises we make, and even the way we understand another person.

A Diary for Strangers

A truly remarkable exploration of the complexity of activism/actionism, courage and emotional caretaking. It’s also funny, dynamic, understanding and wise.

Luvvie with a Rollaboard

What makes Cruising work so well is its confidence. It leans unapologetically into queer culture without feeling the need to over-explain itself. The themes are contemporary, the characters complicated and the storytelling refreshingly intelligent. Funny, gritty, clever and unexpectedly moving, Cruising is fresh Fringe theatre at its best a wonderfully pleasant shock and exactly the kind of unexpected discovery that makes taking a chance at the Fringe worthwhile.

Pure Life Plus

A lot of fun, a piece with a great heart that will make you glad you stepped onboard and went along for the ride. The lighting is superb throught, and along with the soundscape effectively creates the feel of a bustling cruise ship. Katrina Bennett‘s script is full of wonderful lines, providing commentary on the climate emergency while avoiding any heavy preaching.

Everything Theatre
